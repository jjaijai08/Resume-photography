About the work

Based inCabanatuan City, PH

I've always been drawn to moments, the planned ones and the ones that just happen. Whether I'm covering an event or simply out with no agenda, my camera is usually with me, ready for whatever the day decides to show me.

In my free time, I like to wander and explore, using photography as a reason to slow down and actually look at the world around me. For me, it's less about the perfect shot and more about being present enough to catch something real.
